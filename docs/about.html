PDRC tech for everyone.

We're a deep-tech materials startup in Tempe, Arizona, taking passive daytime radiative cooling out of research labs and into a can of paint anyone can use. CryoPaint is made in Ohio, supporting US jobs.

Innovative cooling

CryoPaint® is a water-based paint that actively cools outdoor surfaces up to 15°F below air temperature, with no emissions or moving parts.

Built for the field

Scalable, field-ready solutions for vehicles, housing, logistics and energy infrastructure.

Arizona roots

We develop in one of the hottest regions in the US, the perfect testing ground, with hundreds of hours of testing in Arizona sun.

Benefits that matter

Cooler surfaces, instantly

Up to 15°F below air temperature around the clock, with no maintenance. Works as soon as it's applied.

Lower cooling loads

Removes solar gain at the surface of low-insulation enclosures, vehicles and buildings. Pay once, save on cooling right away.

Equipment protection

Extends the life of parts that fail from heat stress, like gaskets, bearings and electronics, and cuts thermal cycling damage.

Passive by design

No moving parts. Heat flows from the warm surface to the cold sky and out to deep space, per the 2nd law of thermodynamics.

Low-VOC, water-based

Safer and easier to work with than solvent-based coatings. Cleans up with water.

Best when it's hottest

The hotter and sunnier it gets, the bigger the advantage. Made for the desert.

Built for the real world

Fleet vehicles

Less radiant heat through the roof, more comfortable cabins, less load on the HVAC.

Mobile homes and RVs

Lower cooling demand, more comfort, and a roof protected from thermal fatigue.

Shipping containers

Passively lowers internal temps, better than insulation and cheaper than a mini split.

HVAC and AC units

Cuts solar gain on rooftop housings and cabinets. Never coat the coils or fins.

Outdoor electronics

Keeps radios, sensors and infrastructure nodes from overheating by cooling their enclosures.

Pilot infrastructure

Edge utilities, transit shelters, microgrids and desert operations on experimental applications.

Milestones

  1. 2021

    Founded

    Set up to bring radiative cooling from research labs to real-world use.

  2. 2022

    CryoSkin launch

    A cooling vinyl film for phones and vehicle surfaces, our first consumer PDRC product. Discontinued in 2024.

  3. 2023

    CryoPaint R&D begins

    Work starts on a sprayable PDRC coating. Same year: winning concept in the Innovate Phoenix Challenge, a chilled drinking fountain with CryoSkin on its shade.

  4. 2025

    CryoPaint commercial launch

    The first direct-to-consumer passive daytime radiative cooling paint, launched summer 2025.

  5. 2026

    Popular Science award

    Named to the 2026 Popular Science Home of the Future Awards.

Products and prototypes

CryoPaint® Flagship

A sprayable cooling paint for any outdoor surface, up to 15°F below air temperature.

CryoCan™ In development

An aerosol spray-can version of CryoPaint for portable, on-the-go application.

CryoTile™ Case

Prototype concept. A magnetically mounted phone case using PDRC for device cooling. Never went to full production.

CryoSkin

Discontinued in 2024. A reflective vinyl film for mobile and vehicle surfaces.

In the field and in the news

Commercial pilots

Coated commercial roof sections in real-world conditions, with significant surface-temperature drops that improved comfort below.

Municipal programs

Piloted a chilled water fountain with the City of Phoenix Office of Innovation.

Industry collaborations
